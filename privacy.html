我们不收集任何个人数据。

《一刀两半》不需要注册或登录，不包含广告，不包含第三方统计或追踪代码，也不会把任何信息发送到我们的服务器。

儿童：本游戏不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

English

We do not collect any personal data.

Halve It needs no account or sign-in. It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking code, and it does not send any information to our servers.

Stored on your device: your best Endless score and today's Daily Five result. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Sharing: the result text is created on your device. It leaves it only when you tap share and pick a destination in the system share sheet.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes: if ads, leaderboards or other features that process data are added later, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.
