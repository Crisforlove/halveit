怎么玩

在屏幕上划一刀，把形状切成面积相等的两半。松手后会显示两边各占百分之几，离 50% 越近越好。

常见问题

每日五刀是什么？
每天所有玩家拿到同样的五个形状，每天只能切一次，成绩是五刀误差的总和。日期按世界协调时（UTC）切换。

无尽模式是什么？
形状一个接一个出，误差超过容许值扣一颗心，三颗心用完结束。容许值会越来越小。

我的记录会丢吗？
最高分和当天的成绩只保存在这台设备上。删除 App 会一并清除。

How to play

Swipe across the shape to cut it into two halves of equal area. When you let go, the game shows what share each side got. The closer to 50%, the better.

FAQ

What is Daily Five?
Every player gets the same five shapes each day, and you can play them once. Your result is the total error over the five cuts. The day changes at midnight UTC.

What is Endless?
Shapes keep coming. A cut that misses by more than the allowed margin costs a heart, and the game ends when all three are gone. The margin gets tighter as you go.

Can I lose my progress?
Your best score and today's result are stored only on this device. Deleting the app removes them.
